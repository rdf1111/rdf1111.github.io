videoEditingStudio turns your own recordings into finished vertical short videos — subtitles, narration, music, titles and cuts — and publishes them to the YouTube channel, TikTok account or Instagram professional account you connect. Everything runs on your PC: there is no videoEditingStudio server and no account to create with us.

Download for Windows (free)

Features

  • Subtitles. Recognises speech in your video on your PC and lets you edit every subtitle line and its timing.
  • Highlight shorts. Cuts a longer recording into short vertical clips with a title and captions.
  • Auto shorts. Builds a short from your clips with a script, narration, background music and a style template, then lets you edit it on a timeline.
  • Product shorts. Write a short product introduction from product information and clips you choose: pick the exact ranges of each clip, review the scene order and script, add your own notes, then make the short.
  • Thumbnails. Design a cover image for each short.
  • Publishing and calendar. Publish a finished short to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram from inside the app, and see what you published on an upload calendar.

How it works

  1. Download the app and unzip it. No sign-up is needed.
  2. Add your own video files (or clips you are allowed to use) and choose a short type.
  3. Edit the subtitles, script, scenes, music and title in the editor.
  4. Export the finished short to your PC, or connect your own account and press Publish.

Video, audio and screen content are processed on your PC. Text of a script you write may be sent to the speech service you choose when you create narration.

Publishing to your accounts

YouTube

Uses YouTube API Services. You sign in with Google in your own browser. The app asks for youtube.readonly (to show your channel name and check the status of videos you uploaded with the app) and youtube.upload (to upload the video you selected when you press publish). It does not edit or delete existing videos or read comments. By using the YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

Uses the TikTok Content Posting API with TikTok Login Kit. You sign in with TikTok in your own browser. The app asks for user.info.basic (to show the name of the account you connected) and video.publish (to post the video you selected, with the caption and privacy level you chose, when you press publish). It does not read or change anything else on your account. By using the TikTok features you agree to the TikTok Terms of Service; see also the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Instagram

Uses the Instagram API with Instagram Login. You sign in with your Instagram professional account in your own browser. The app asks for instagram_business_basic (to show your account) and instagram_business_content_publish (to post the video you selected as a Reel when you press publish). It does not read your messages, comments, followers or insights. Instagram has no private posts through this API, so a posted Reel is public.

See the Privacy Policy for what is stored on your PC and how to disconnect an account.

Download

Free for Windows 10/11 (64-bit): videoEditingStudio releases. Unzip the archive and run SubtitleStudio.exe. The first speech recognition downloads a speech model to your PC. The build is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm with "More info" and "Run anyway".

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, about 3 GB of free disk space. A YouTube channel, TikTok account or Instagram professional account is needed only for publishing.
