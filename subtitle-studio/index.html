What it is

videoEditingStudio is a desktop application for Windows. It turns your own video recordings into short vertical videos with subtitles, and can upload the finished video to your own YouTube channel, TikTok account or Instagram professional account without leaving the app.

The app runs on your PC. Video, audio and screen content are processed locally; there is no videoEditingStudio server and no account to create with us.

What it does

  • Subtitles. Recognises speech in your video on your PC and lets you edit the resulting subtitles.
  • Highlight shorts. Cuts a longer recording into short vertical clips with a title and captions.
  • Auto shorts. Builds a short from your clips with a script, narration, background music and a style template that you can then edit on a timeline.
  • Upload to YouTube. Sends a finished short to the YouTube channel you connected, with the title, description, tags, visibility, audience setting and optional schedule you entered, and shows the resulting video link and status.
  • Post to TikTok. Sends a finished short to the TikTok account you connected, with a caption built from your title, description and tags and the privacy level you chose, and shows the resulting status.

How it uses YouTube

The upload feature uses YouTube API Services. You sign in with Google in your own browser and choose what to allow. The app asks for two permissions only:

PermissionWhy the app needs it
youtube.readonlyTo show the name of the channel you connected and to check the processing and visibility status of videos you uploaded with the app.
youtube.uploadTo upload the video you selected to your channel when you press the publish button.

The app uploads only when you press publish. It does not edit or delete existing videos, read comments, or act on any other part of your account. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and how to disconnect.

By using the YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How it uses Instagram

The Instagram feature uses the Instagram Graph API content publishing with Facebook Login. You sign in with Facebook in your own browser and choose the Page and Instagram professional account to allow. The app uses the permissions instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management only to find that account and to post the video you selected as a Reel when you press publish. It does not read your messages, comments, followers or insights. See the Privacy Policy.

How it uses TikTok

The TikTok feature uses the TikTok Content Posting API with TikTok Login Kit. You sign in with TikTok in your own browser and choose what to allow. The app asks for two permissions only: user.info.basic (to show the name of the account you connected) and video.publish (to post the video you selected when you press publish). It does not read or change anything else on your account. Until the app has passed the TikTok audit, TikTok keeps every video posted through it private. By using the TikTok features you agree to the TikTok Terms of Service; TikTok's handling of your data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Download

Free download for Windows 10/11 (64-bit): videoEditingStudio releases (zip, about 370 MB). Unzip and run SubtitleStudio.exe. No account or sign-up is needed. The current version is 2026.10.06. The first speech recognition downloads the Whisper model (about 1.5 GB) to your PC. The build is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm with "More info" and "Run anyway".

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, about 3 GB of free disk space. A YouTube channel or a TikTok account is needed only for the publishing features.
