Summary

videoEditingStudio is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows PC. We, the developer, do not operate a server for it and do not receive your videos, your Google or TikTok account information, or your YouTube or TikTok data. What the app keeps, it keeps on your PC.

YouTube API Services

The upload feature of the app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

The TikTok publish feature uses the TikTok Content Posting API through TikTok Login Kit. By using it you also agree to the TikTok Terms of Service; how TikTok handles your information is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy. The app asks TikTok for two permissions: your basic profile (user.info.basic: display name and avatar) and posting videos (video.publish). Until the app has passed the TikTok audit, TikTok keeps every video posted through it private; the app reports that status as it is.

The app never sees or stores your Google or TikTok password. You sign in on the Google or TikTok sign-in page in your own browser.

The app does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, watch history or analytics.

How the information is used and shared

  • It is used only to show you which channel is connected, to upload the video you chose, and to show you the result.
  • It is sent only between your PC and Google, or your PC and TikTok. It is not sent to the developer, and it is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any other party.
  • It is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Other features of the app

Separately from YouTube, some editing features send text only to outside services when you use them: script text may be sent to Claude (Anthropic) through your own Claude subscription to draft or refine a script, and narration text is sent to the Microsoft Edge text-to-speech service to produce a voice. Your video, audio and screen content stay on your PC, and no YouTube API data is sent to these services.

Advertising, cookies and tracking

The app shows no advertising and does not let third parties serve content. It places no cookies and contains no analytics or tracking. The only information it stores on your device is what is listed above, plus your own projects and settings.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • In the app, open 채널 연동 (Channel connections) and press 연동 해제 (Disconnect) on the channel. The app asks Google or TikTok to revoke its token and deletes the encrypted connection file from your PC.
  • For TikTok you can also remove the app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions in the TikTok app.
  • You can also revoke the access of the app at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Publish records and drafts are ordinary files in your export folder and can be deleted there. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

The app confirms that a connection is still authorised whenever it uses it; if access has been revoked, the app marks the channel as needing to be connected again and stops using it.

Children

The app is a tool for video creators and is not directed at children.
